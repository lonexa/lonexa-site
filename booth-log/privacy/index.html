Privacy Policy — Booth Log

Booth Log Last updated: 2026-09-07

Booth Log is published by Lonexa LLC.

What we collect

Nothing.

Booth Log has no account and no sign-in. Everything you enter is stored in a database on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see your data, we cannot recover it, and we have no copy of it.

There are no ads, no advertising identifier, and no analytics SDK.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a diagnostic report may be sent to our error-tracking provider (Sentry) so the fault can be fixed. These reports contain technical information about the failure — the app version, the device model, and where in the code the error happened. They do not contain the records you entered.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play and by RevenueCat, which records that a purchase happened so the app can unlock. We never see your payment details.

Your data

Because your records never leave your device, deleting the app deletes them. Export first if you want to keep them — the app can produce a PDF and a CSV of everything it holds.
