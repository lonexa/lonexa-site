Privacy Policy — Donated

Donated Last updated: 2026-10-31

Donated is published by Lonexa LLC.

What we collect

None of your records.

Donated has no account and no sign-in. The charities, dates, items, values and photos you record are kept in a database and a folder on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see them, we cannot recover them, and we hold no copy.

There are no ads, no advertising identifier and no analytics SDK.

The camera and your photos

The app asks to use the camera only when you choose to photograph what you are giving or a charity's receipt. Each photo is copied into the app's own storage on your phone. When you choose a photo you already took, Android hands the app that one picture and nothing else; the app does not read your gallery.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a diagnostic report may be sent to our error-tracking provider (Sentry) so the fault can be fixed. It contains technical information about the failure: the app version, the device model and where in the code the error happened. It does not contain your records or your photos.

Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is handled by Google Play and by RevenueCat, which records that a purchase happened, against an anonymous identifier, so the app can unlock. We never see your name or payment details.

Your data

Because your records never leave your phone, uninstalling the app deletes them, and so does Settings → Start fresh. Export first if you want to keep them: the app produces a CSV of everything it holds, free, at any time.
