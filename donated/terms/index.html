What this app is

Donated is a record-keeping tool for noncash charitable gifts. It is not tax, legal or accounting advice, and it is not tax software.

It adds up the values you record. Where it suggests a value, the figure comes from The Salvation Army Donation Value Guide, which the app names and dates; The Salvation Army does not set the value of anyone's donation, and neither do we. You choose every value, and you are responsible for it.

Where the app mentions an IRS threshold, it quotes and links the IRS publication it comes from. It does not decide whether you may take a deduction, whether you should itemize, or what you owe, and it files nothing with anyone. Those questions are for you and whoever prepares your return. Published guides and IRS rules change; confirm anything that matters against the source.
