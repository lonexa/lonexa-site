Steady Increment

Android · In development

A gym training log for lifting and machine cardio. Every recommendation comes from a deterministic engine running on the phone and is snapped to weights the equipment in front of you can physically make. It records, it computes, and it does both with no connection at all.

Android · In development

A booth log for market and craft-fair vendors: what stock went out, what sold, and what the day actually paid once the pitch, the petrol and the materials came out of it. It works in a hall with no signal, because that is where the selling happens.

MicroMajors

Android · In development

A baseball management game. You train a roster of players the way you would tend a garden — slowly, and without ever being told what they are capable of — then set a lineup and send them out. Matches are simulated by a deterministic engine on our server and played back to you as animated pixel art with a full box score. It needs an account and a connection, because a game between two people’s teams cannot be decided by either person’s phone.
