## What this app is

Tubkeeper is a log and a calculator for hot tub and swim spa water. **It does
not test water, and it does not certify that water is safe to use.**

The target ranges it shows are copied from a hot tub manufacturer's published
water-care guidance, and the limits for using the tub from the U.S. Centers for
Disease Control and Prevention. The app names and links each source. The
amounts it suggests are arithmetic from the volume you enter, the percentage
printed on your product's label, and the readings you log. They are only as
good as those numbers: a wrong volume, a misread strip or a different product
gives a wrong amount.

Read and follow the label of every product you use. Where a label and the app
disagree, follow the label. Add one product at a time, keep chemicals away from
children, never mix products before they go in the water, and do not use the
tub until a fresh test reads within range. If you are unsure, ask your dealer
or a pool and spa professional.
