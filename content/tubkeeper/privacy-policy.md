# Privacy Policy — Tubkeeper

**Tubkeeper**
Last updated: 2026-10-31

Tubkeeper is published by Lonexa LLC.

## What we collect

**None of your records.**

Tubkeeper has no account and no sign-in. Your tub's size, the test readings you
log, what you add to the water, and the chores you record are kept in a database
on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see them,
we cannot recover them, and we hold no copy.

There are no ads, no advertising identifier and no analytics SDK. The app does
not use your location, camera, contacts or photos.

## Reminders

If you switch reminders on, the app asks Android for permission to show
notifications, and schedules them on your phone. They are not sent from a
server; there is no server.

## Crash reports

If the app crashes, a diagnostic report may be sent to our error-tracking
provider (Sentry) so the fault can be fixed. It contains technical information
about the failure: the app version, the device model and where in the code the
error happened. It does not contain your readings or any other record.

## Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is handled by Google Play and by RevenueCat, which
records that a purchase happened, against an anonymous identifier, so the app
can unlock. We never see your name or payment details.

## Your data

Because your records never leave your phone, uninstalling the app deletes them,
and so does Settings → Start fresh. Export first if you want to keep them: the
app produces CSV files of everything it holds, free, at any time.
