## What this app is

Booth Log is a record-keeping tool. **It is not professional advice, and it is
not accounting or tax software.**

It performs arithmetic on figures you enter: what you sold, what your materials
cost, what the pitch cost, how far you travelled and how long you worked. Every
number it shows is derived from those entries and from nothing else, and the
app shows the calculation alongside the result so you can check it.

It does not file anything, does not submit anything to any authority, and does
not tell you what you owe. Whether a figure it displays is the right one to put
on a tax return, and what your obligations are, is between you and whoever
advises you.

Booth Log cannot know about a sale you did not record, and its stock
reconciliation reports a discrepancy without claiming to explain it.
