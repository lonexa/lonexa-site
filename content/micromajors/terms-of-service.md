## 1. What this is

MicroMajors is a baseball management game. You train a roster of fictional
players, set a lineup, and play matches against other people's teams. The matches
are simulated by a deterministic engine on our server and you watch them back as
an animated replay with a box score.

**Everything in it is fiction.** The players, their names, the teams, the
stadiums and the leagues are generated by the game. Nothing here is a real
person, a real club, or a real competition, and no real league, team or player is
endorsed by or affiliated with us.

**It is a game, not a sportsbook.** There is no wagering, no cash prize, and
nothing you win can be exchanged for money or anything of value outside the game.

## 3. The simulation is the referee, and it is final

Every match is decided by the engine on our server, from your roster, your
lineup, your opponent's, and a random seed. **The result it produces is final.**

- The engine is the same code for everybody and it does not know who you are.
- Your device does not compute results, and no result your device claims is
  accepted. This is what stops the game being decided by whoever is willing to
  modify their app.
- We do not sell an advantage. Everything purchasable with money is also
  obtainable by playing.

Attempting to alter, forge or replay a result, or to reach the server other than
through the app, ends the account.
